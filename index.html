The Issue

Ontarians say yes. The system doesn’t always listen.

1,776 Ontarians waiting for an organ transplant As of Oct. 18, 2026 · Trillium Gift of Life Network (2026) One Ontarian dies waiting every three days.

Today, families are asked to reaffirm a registered donor’s decision at one of the hardest moments of their lives. Our proposal takes that weight off them.

Our Ask

Opt-out donation for Ontarians 16 and older, with no family veto.

Ontario would treat every eligible adult and teen 16 and older as a donor unless they opt out, and their decision would stand.

  • Deemed consent

    Opt-out for everyone 16 and older

    Every eligible Ontarian aged 16 and older is considered a donor unless they choose to opt out.

  • Final decision

    No family veto

    A registered or deemed decision to donate cannot be overridden by family members.

  • Your choice

    Opt out any time

    Change your decision whenever you want, online or in person at ServiceOntario.

  • Safeguards

    Clear exemptions

    People under 16, people without the capacity to decide, and people who have lived in Ontario for less than 12 months are not deemed donors.

  • Before it starts

    An 18-month education period

    The law takes effect after 18 months of public education, so every Ontarian knows how to register or opt out.

  • Capacity

    Funding for the donation system

    Hospitals and donation teams get the funding they need to support more donations.

Nobody is required to donate. Saying no stays as simple as saying yes, and you can change your decision at any time.

Petition

Paper signatures are the ones that count.

The Legislative Assembly of Ontario accepts paper petitions with original, handwritten signatures. Our goal is 10,000.

  1. Print the petition

    It’s one letter-size page with 12 signature lines. The petition text sits at the top of every page, as the Assembly requires. Download the petition (PDF)

  2. Collect original signatures

    Each signer prints their name and address and signs by hand. Signers must live in Ontario and can be under 18. Photocopies and online signatures don’t count.

  3. Return your pages

    Bring completed pages to a signing table. A sponsoring MPP presents them at Queen’s Park.

Petition To the Legislative Assembly of Ontario:

WHEREAS 1,776 Ontarians were waiting for an organ transplant as of Oct. 18, 2026, and one Ontarian dies every three days while waiting;

WHEREAS nine in 10 Ontarians support organ donation, but only 34 per cent are registered donors;

WHEREAS families in Ontario are asked to reaffirm a registered donor’s decision, and a registered decision to donate can still go unfulfilled when a family says no;

WHEREAS Ontarians can register their decision to donate from the age of 16;

We, the undersigned, petition the Legislative Assembly of Ontario to pass legislation establishing an opt-out system of organ and tissue donation for Ontarians aged 16 and older, under which a person’s registered or deemed decision to donate cannot be overridden by family members.
